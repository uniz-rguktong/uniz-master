# Phone screenshots for Play Console

Add **2–8** PNG or JPEG screenshots (min 320px short edge; 16:9 or 9:16 phone aspect).

## Recommended captures

| # | Screen | How to capture |
|---|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Sign-in | https://uniz.rguktong.in/student/signin — mobile viewport |
| 2 | Student home | Log in → home dashboard |
| 3 | Academics | Grades / attendance tab |
| 4 | Notifications | Campus updates or alerts |
| 5 | Outpass (optional) | Request / status screen |
| 6 | Privacy (optional) | https://uniz.rguktong.in/privacy |

## Tips

- Use **Chrome DevTools** → device toolbar → iPhone or Pixel preset → capture screenshot.
- Or take real photos from an Android phone after internal testing (Phase D).
- File names: `01-sign-in.png`, `02-home.png`, etc.
