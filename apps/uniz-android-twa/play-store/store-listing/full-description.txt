uniZ is the official campus platform for RGUKT Ongole students and staff. Access academics, attendance, semester registration, outpass requests, campus notifications, and more — all in one place.

FOR STUDENTS
• View grades, CGPA, and semester results
• Track attendance by subject and day
• Register for semesters and manage academic records
• Request outpass and track approval status
• Receive push alerts for campus updates and deadlines
• Browse institute news, notifications, and website content

FOR STAFF & ADMIN
• Secure staff portal for campus operations
• Bulk academic uploads and student records
• Campus communications and push alerts
• Role-based access for deans, HODs, webmasters, and security

BUILT FOR RGUKT ONGOLE
uniZ is designed around how RGUKT Ongole runs day to day — from classroom attendance to hostel outpass workflows. Sign in with your college credentials and get a fast, mobile-friendly experience tuned for campus life.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• HTTPS-only; college account sign-in
• Privacy policy: https://uniz.rguktong.in/privacy
• Academic data used only for app functionality — not sold to third parties

REQUIREMENTS
• Active RGUKT Ongole student or staff account
• Internet connection for sync and notifications

Support: webadmin@rguktong.ac.in
