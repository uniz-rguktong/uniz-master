# Profile

## Student

`D210001` (Asha Demo, CSE, E4, section A) is the populated fixture. `D210002` (Nila Demo) has a profile and no academic rows.

1. `/student` shows the greeting, quick actions, the profile card, the campus notice board, and the upcoming examination from the seating row (Demo Operating Systems, hall LH-1).
2. Edit profile turns the personal fields into inputs: full name, gender, blood group, phone, and date of birth, with Cancel and Save. In this capture the gender control shows `F`.
3. `/student/resetpassword` is the password form. A weak or mismatched value stays on the form.
4. `/student/grievance` is the grievance form. Submitting it empty toasts that the fields are required. A fictional pending grievance was inserted for `D210001`; this page is the submit form, not a list of past grievances.
5. `/student/help` is help and support.
6. Faculty `FACULTY_CSE` (Dr Meera Demo) uses `/faculty`: overview, My Profile, Branch Students (CSE), and Change Password. Below the `md` breakpoint the faculty sidebar is hidden until the menu control is opened.
7. HOD, dean, director, webadmin, and CoE share admin shells. Student Details for `hod_cse` lists both demo students and is read-only for that department. Webadmin and dean also have Student Bulk Ops and Faculty Management.
8. `/admin/profile` is the signed-in admin profile route.

The home cards for attendance and grade points on the student dashboard stayed at `--` in this capture. The subject figures appear after Get Results and Get Attendance. See [Grades](grades.md) and [Attendance](attendance.md).
